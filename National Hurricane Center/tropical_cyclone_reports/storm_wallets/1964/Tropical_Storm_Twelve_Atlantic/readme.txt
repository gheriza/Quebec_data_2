TROPICAL STORM 12   November 4-9, 1964

The following is the directory structure used for tropical storm 12 in the
Hurricane Wallet Digital Archives.  The first level of subdirectory is the 
year (e.g. ATL1964) and all directories subordinate will have data for storms
which occurred in that year.  The README.TXT file in the ATL1964
directory is the directory structure for the 1964 digital archives.

The second level of subdirectories are labeled by the storm name.  The 1964
storms were unnamed Tropical Storm (TS) One, TS2, Abby, Brenda, Cleo, Dora,
Ethel, Florence, Gladys, Hilda, Isbell, and TS12.  The README.TXT file in
this directory lists and summarizes the files archived for tropical storm 12.

The third level of directories are labeled by a name, acronym, or mnemonic
related to the data contained in that directory. Types of files are: *.GIF files
are graphics files, *.TXT files are ASCII text files, and *.WPD files are in
WordPerfect 6.1.
