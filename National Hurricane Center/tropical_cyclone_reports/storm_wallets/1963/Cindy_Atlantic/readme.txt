HURRICANE CINDY   September 16-20, 1963

The following is the directory structure used for hurricane CINDY in the
Hurricane Wallet Digital Archives.  The first level of subdirectory is the 
year (e.g. ATL1963) and all directories subordinate will have data for storms
which occurred in that year.  The README.TXT file in the ATL1963
directory is the directory structure for the 1963 digital archives.

The second level of subdirectories are labeled by the storm name.  The 1963
storms were Arlene, Beulah, Cindy, Debra, Edith, Tropical Storm 6 (TS6),
Flora, Ginny, Helena.  The README.TXT file in this directory lists and
summarizes the files archived for tropical storm CINDY.

The third level of directories are labeled by a name, acronym, or mnemonic
related to the data contained in that directory. Types of files are: *.GIF files
are graphics files, *.TXT files are ASCII text files, and *.WPD files are in
WordPerfect 6.1.
