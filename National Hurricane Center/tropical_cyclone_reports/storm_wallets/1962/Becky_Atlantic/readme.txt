TROPICAL STORM BECKY   August 26-September 1, 1962

The following is the directory structure used for tropical storm BECKY in the
Hurricane Wallet Digital Archives.  The first level of subdirectory is the 
year (e.g. ATL1962) and all directories subordinate will have data for storms
which occurred in that year.  The README.TXT file in the ATL1962
directory is the directory structure for the 1962 digital archives.

The second level of subdirectories are labeled by the storm name.  In 1962 we
had Alma, Becky, Celia, Daisy, and Ella.  The README.TXT file in this
directory lists and summarizes the files archived for tropical storm BECKY.

The third level of directories are labeled by a name, acronym, or mnemonic
related to the data contained in that directory. Types of files are: *.GIF files
are graphics files, *.TXT files are ASCII text files, and *.WPD files are in
WordPerfect 6.1.
